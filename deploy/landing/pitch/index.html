PROBLEM AND MARKET

Clinics want AI, but can't trust it with women's health data

~50,800

OB-GYNs practicing in the US, with a shortage projected by 2030. Their visit time is scarce, and history-taking eats into it.

3.6M

US births a year. Each one is months of prenatal, postpartum and newborn visits: the path from gynecology into maternal and child care.

Why now

Reproductive data carries legal risk in some states. AI gateways with privacy floors and per-call receipts now let a clinic check what happened.

Sources: US HHS workforce projection, 2021 (OB-GYN count for 2018, as reported by The Hill); CDC NCHS, Births in the United States, 2024 (3,628,934 births). Revenue model: monthly fee per clinician.
